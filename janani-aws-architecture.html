1. AWS Strands Agents SDK

Voice-Controlled Chatbot & Clinical Tool Orchestrator

Slide Feature: 🎙️ Voice Chatbot
HOW IT IS USED IN JANANI SETU

The AWS Strands Agent acts as the intelligent clinical conductor. Instead of just answering questions with generic LLM knowledge, it dynamically evaluates the user's intent and autonomously calls specialized tools to retrieve patient vitals, search nutrition data, verify government schemes, and trigger isolated risk models.

  • Empathetic Persona: Tuned as a caring older sister / trained ASHA worker (Janani).
  • 12 Indian Languages: Speaks Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, and Hinglish.
  • Autonomous Tools: Invokes DynamoDB for vitals, OpenSearch for RAG, and Firecracker for emergency triage.
1 Mother speaks: "Mujhe chakkar aa rahe hain" (I feel dizzy)
↓
2 Strands Agent detects pregnancy symptoms & checks Cedar auth
↓
3 Calls get_patient_health_summary + search_health_knowledge
↓
4 Synthesizes clear, spoken Hindi advice grounded in clinical facts

2. Amazon OpenSearch Service

Health Knowledge RAG & Semantic Nutrition Retrieval

Slide Feature: 🥗 Smart Nutrition & Schemes
HOW IT IS USED IN JANANI SETU

OpenSearch powers the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) knowledge repository. Large Language Models often hallucinate medical guidelines or fail on regional Indian dietary dishes. OpenSearch indexes verified clinical documents, regional Indian food recipes, and welfare programs so the agent only quotes factual, validated medical advice.

  • 1,014 Indian Foods: Exact Calories, Protein, Iron, Calcium, and Folate per dish (Dal, Palak, Roti, Khichdi).
  • 120 Pregnancy Articles: Safe exercise, fetal development, and trimester guidelines.
  • Government Schemes: Real-time eligibility for PMMVY (₹6,000 cash), JSY (₹1,400 rural), and free IFA tablets.
  • 12 Bilingual Medical Q&As: Curated emergency red-flag guidance in English & Hindi.

↓
2 OpenSearch executes BM25 search across janani-nutrition index
↓
3 Returns top items: Moong dal, Palak, Chana with Iron & Folate mg
↓
4 Agent cites accurate iron content without guessing

3. AWS Cedar Authorization Engine

Fine-Grained Role Privacy (Mother vs ASHA vs Doctor)

Slide Feature: 👥 Tri-Party Role Collaboration
HOW IT IS USED IN JANANI SETU

Maternal data is strictly confidential. AWS Cedar enforces mathematical, fine-grained access control before every single API call or AI tool invocation. It guarantees that a pregnant mother can only see her own records, an ASHA worker can only view assigned village mothers, and doctors have write access to clinical prescriptions.

  • Mother: Can chat freely, view her own vitals, risk scores, and diet logs. FORBIDDEN from seeing other mothers.
  • ASHA Worker: Can view assigned patients, log new vitals, and view risk alerts. FORBIDDEN from altering doctor prescriptions.
  • Doctor: Full read and write authorization for all medical diagnostics.
1 Incoming Request: POST /chat (user_id: user_001, role: Mother)
↓
2 Cedar evaluates: is_authorized(User::"user_001", Action::"Chat", Resource::"AIChat")
↓
3 Decision: cedar_permit → Request proceeds to AI engine
↓
4 If ASHA attempts modifying doctor Rx → Immediate cedar_forbid
File: janani_agent_server/cedar_auth.py

4. Amazon DynamoDB (Local / Cloud)

High-Performance NoSQL Patient Health & ANC Checkup Store

Slide Feature: 📊 Mother Profile & Risk Dashboard
HOW IT IS USED IN JANANI SETU

Replaces bulky relational databases with a fast, serverless NoSQL datastore. DynamoDB stores longitudinal patient health records, Antenatal Care (ANC) visit checkups, blood pressure measurements, and nutrition tracking.

  • JananiPatients Table: Partition Key user_id. Real-time systolic/diastolic BP, blood glucose, gestational week, risk level, and assigned ASHA.
  • JananiVisits Table: Composite Key (user_id + visit_id). Chronological tracking across ANC-1, ANC-2, ANC-3, and ANC-4 checkups.
  • JananiNutritionLogs Table: Daily meal logs with calorie, calcium, and iron tracking.

5. AWS Firecracker MicroVM

Sensitive AI Processing & Hardware-Isolated Clinical Sandboxing

Slide Feature: 🚨 SOS Emergency & Clinical Triage
HOW IT IS USED IN JANANI SETU

Maternal emergencies (such as preeclampsia, eclamptic convulsions, and antepartum hemorrhage) involve highly sensitive Protected Health Information (PHI). AWS Firecracker runs these sensitive workloads inside lightweight, hardware-isolated microVMs with sub-5ms boot times and strict memory confinement.

  • Sub-5ms Boot Latency: Boots an isolated microVM in ~4.2 ms with dedicated vCPU and 128 MiB ceiling.
  • Multi-Factor Obstetric Triage: Detects preeclampsia triad (BP > 140/90 + headache + swelling) → Returns emergency RED triage code.
  • PHI Redaction: Strips Indian phone numbers, 12-digit Aadhaar numbers, and MCP IDs inside the microVM before cloud dispatch.
  • Ephemeral Storage: All memory and scratch files are securely shredded on task termination.

↓
2 Firecracker microVM boots in 4.2ms with 128 MiB boundary
↓
3 Calculates Preeclampsia Risk Score + Flags NSAID Contraindications
↓
4 Emits RED Alert: Urgent hospital referral within 2 hours
